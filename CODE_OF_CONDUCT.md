# Code of Conduct

Savior Systems is committed to a welcoming and harassment‑free environment for contributors and users alike.

## Our Standards
- **Respect** – Treat everyone with courtesy and professionalism.
- **Inclusivity** – Celebrate diverse backgrounds, perspectives, and abilities.
- **Integrity** – No deception, plagiarism, or undisclosed conflicts of interest.
- **Safety** – Do not share or request any personal data, credentials, or private code.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks.
- Threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Spam, trolling, or abusive language.
- Sharing confidential or internal information.

## Enforcement
- Issues are reviewed by a maintainer and, when needed, escalated to the security contact in `SECURITY.md`.
- Violations may result in comment removal, issue/PR closure, or banning from the organization.

By participating, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.
